DEL RIO, Texas — Thirteen children served by CASA of Val Verde County reached permanent homes in September and October, the group's executive director told the Val Verde County Commissioners Court on Wednesday, Nov. 4.

Ana R. Moreno, the nonprofit's [executive director](https://www.casaofvalverde.org/staff), gave the group's end-of-year report to the court, according to the [meeting video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nd0UBOJHozg) (5:54). CASA, or Court Appointed Special Advocates, trains volunteers to advocate for children in the foster care system.

Moreno said four children were adopted through two cases, six were reunified with their mothers through two cases, and three were placed with their maternal grandmother, who is their primary managing conservator. Two more children in one case are moving toward adoption, she said.

As of Sept. 1, CASA was serving 27 children in foster care, Moreno said. After the case closures, and one new case in October, the group now serves 15.

"Our goal isn't simply to close a case, but we want to ensure every child we serve has the opportunity to grow in a safe, stable and nurturing environment," Moreno said.

**Volunteers**

The group trained 14 volunteer advocates this fiscal year in three classes: five in November 2025, five in April 2026 and four in October 2026, Moreno said. Volunteers logged more than 1,000 hours through more than 980 documented advocacy activities, including court hearings, family visits and family engagement meetings, she said.

Many of the children are placed outside Val Verde County, so volunteers often travel long distances, she said.

Moreno thanked the court for the county's financial support and for providing the group's office space. The report was listed on the [meeting agenda](https://www.valverdecounty.texas.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_11042026-809) as item 11, under Commissioner Juan Vazquez. No action was required on the report.

**What's next**

Moreno said CASA will keep reporting its numbers to the court quarterly. She said the group continues to monitor children after cases close because circumstances can change, and she urged residents to learn how they can support children in foster care. More information is available at [casaofvalverde.org](https://www.casaofvalverde.org).
