DEL RIO, Texas — People who missed their Oct. 26, 2026, Del Rio Municipal Court date have been placed on the court's pre-warrant docket and have a 30-day grace period to resolve their cases, according to a City of Del Rio public announcement.

Those on the list must contact the court before Nov. 26, 2026, when warrants will be issued, according to the city's [announcement on its Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/CityofDelRio) on Nov. 2. Penalties can include a warrant fee of up to $75.

Payment plans, fine reductions and credit for time served may be available to those who qualify, the city said.

The city has published a [Pre-Warrant List](https://www.cityofdelrio.com/home/showdocument?id=12997&t=639292132176678577) so residents can check whether their names appear.

The court also has a Safe Harbor initiative for Class C misdemeanor cases. Under that policy, a person with a Del Rio Municipal Court warrant for a Class C misdemeanor can come to the court to discuss the case without fear of arrest on that warrant, according to the [court's warrants page](https://www.cityofdelrio.com/government/departments/municipal-court/warrants). Walk-ins may speak with the judge Tuesdays and Thursdays at 9 a.m.

Defendants who cannot attend the regular dockets can request an evening or virtual hearing, according to the [Municipal Court page](https://www.cityofdelrio.com/government/departments/municipal-court).

The Del Rio Municipal Court is at 125 E. Martin St., Del Rio, TX 78840. It can be reached at (830) 774-8506 or municipalcourt@cityofdelrio.com. Cases can also be looked up and paid through the city's [online portal](https://delriotx.municipalonlinepayments.com/delriotx/court/search).
