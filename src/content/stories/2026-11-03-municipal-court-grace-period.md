DEL RIO, Texas — People who missed a scheduled Del Rio Municipal Court appearance should contact the court as soon as possible, according to the court's published policies.

[UNVERIFIED — confirm with court before publishing: A social media post said defendants who missed their Oct. 26, 2026, court date have 30 days to resolve their cases before a warrant is issued.]

According to the [court's warrants page](https://www.cityofdelrio.com/government/departments/municipal-court/warrants), failing to appear for a scheduled court date or meet a deadline can result in an arrest warrant. "To avoid arrest, you must immediately resolve this matter by contacting the Municipal Court," the page states.

The court has a Safe Harbor initiative for Class C misdemeanor cases. Under that policy, a person with a Del Rio Municipal Court warrant for a Class C misdemeanor can appear at the court to discuss the case "without fear of arrest." The page says walk-ins may speak to the judge Tuesdays and Thursdays at 9 a.m.

Municipal Judge Kassandra Flores said at a public presentation earlier this year, "We will never arrest you, we will never issue a warrant if you're in communication with us," according to [The 830 Times](https://830times.com/news-municipal-court-announces-warrant-roundup/). She said the court planned a warrant roundup at the end of October 2026.

Defendants who cannot attend the regular Initial Appearance or Show Cause dockets can ask to be placed on the evening court docket. Virtual hearings are also available on request, according to the [Municipal Court page](https://www.cityofdelrio.com/government/departments/municipal-court).

The court is at 125 E. Martin St. and can be reached at 830-774-8691. Cases can also be looked up and paid through the city's [online portal](https://delriotx.municipalonlinepayments.com/delriotx/court/search).
