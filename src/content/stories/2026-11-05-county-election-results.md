DEL RIO, Texas — County Judge Lewis G. Owens Jr. won another term Tuesday, Nov. 3, and Republican Mary Jane Ross won the district clerk race, according to unofficial results released by the Val Verde County Clerk Elections office.

Owens, a Democrat, received 7,572 votes to 4,047 for Republican Robert Beau Nettleton, according to the county's [summary results report](https://www.valverdecounty.texas.gov/DocumentCenter/View/11107/November-3-2026-General-Election---Unofficial-Results), generated at 10:49 p.m. Tuesday with all ballots counted. Turnout was 38.60%, with 11,980 ballots cast out of 31,038 registered voters.

**District clerk**

Ross defeated Democrat Rachel Magallanes 6,026 to 5,449 in the closest countywide race on the local ballot, according to the report.

**Commissioners Court**

Incumbent Precinct 2 Commissioner Juan Carlos Vazquez, a Democrat, defeated Republican Rey Robles 1,361 to 892. Incumbent Precinct 4 Commissioner Gustavo (Gus) Flores, a Democrat, edged Republican John I. Wardlaw 1,431 to 1,352.

**Other county offices**

Unopposed candidates included County Court at Law Judge Sergio J. Gonzalez with 8,792 votes; County Clerk Teresa Esther Chapoy with 8,039; County Treasurer Aaron D. Rodriguez with 8,381; County Surveyor Abner Martinez with 8,156; and 83rd District Judge Robert E. Cadena with 8,360, according to the report.

**Justices of the peace**

- Precinct 1: Democrat Jesse James Trevino, 1,624, over Republican Mario Bosquez, 785.
- Precinct 2: Democrat Robert "Bobby" Hernandez, 1,312, over Republican J. Randel Dissler, 822, and write-in James Fernandez, 117.
- Precinct 3: Republican Ashly Vicuna, 2,727, over Democrat Loida S. Arellano, 1,412.
- Precinct 4: Democrat Hilda Castillo Lopez, unopposed, 2,154.

**What's next**

The results remain unofficial until the Val Verde County Commissioners Court canvasses the vote later this month. The next regular Commissioners Court meeting is scheduled for Nov. 18, according to the court's [Nov. 4 agenda](https://www.valverdecounty.texas.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_11042026-809).
