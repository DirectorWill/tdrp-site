DEL RIO, Texas — The Val Verde County Commissioners Court on Wednesday, Nov. 4, voted 5-0 to authorize County Judge Lewis G. Owens Jr. to sign a revised application for a $4,848,870.84 state Defense Economic Adjustment Assistance Grant for an aerospace and manufacturing initiative at Laughlin Air Force Base.

The request is $146,351.90 more than the $4,702,518.94 application the court approved Oct. 21, according to the [Nov. 4](https://www.valverdecounty.texas.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_11042026-809) and [Oct. 21](https://www.valverdecounty.texas.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_10162026-808) meeting agendas. The item also authorizes Owens to sign the court's authorized-representative resolutions for the grant.

Presenting the item, a county representative told the court the project total had increased by about $150,000, and that the county still had time before a Nov. 6 deadline to submit a new resolution with the correct amount, according to the [meeting video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nd0UBOJHozg) (14:19).

**Name change**

The representative also said that, on the recommendation of the state, the county was asked to rename the project so it no longer includes "Val Verde," because the current name gave the impression the work would take place off the base. The Nov. 4 agenda lists the project as the "Val Verde Aerospace and Manufacturing Innovation Initiative at Laughlin Airforce Base." The Oct. 21 agenda listed it as the "Val Verde Aerospace and Manufacturing & CTE Innovation Initiative."

Commissioner Juan Vazquez made the motion and Commissioner Fernando Garcia seconded it. Commissioners Kerr Wardlaw and Gustavo Flores and Owens also voted in favor. There was no discussion or dissent.

**Background**

When the court approved the first application Oct. 21, County Engineer Carlos Velarde said the money would buy three pieces of equipment, including a metal 3D printer, so the base could make parts for aging aircraft.

The [Defense Economic Adjustment Assistance Grant](https://gov.texas.gov/military/page/deaag) program is run by the Texas Military Preparedness Commission in the Office of the Governor. It helps communities near military installations with projects that support the bases.
